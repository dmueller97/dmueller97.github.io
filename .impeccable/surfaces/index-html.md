# Surface: index.html (Materialübersicht)

Mode: Read (materials index). Audience: students Kl. 5–12 at home and on classroom devices. Job: find subject → year → topic → material in seconds. Constraints: kurse.js format unchanged; empty years/topics hidden; Impressum/Datenschutz in footer; search with `/`; editor warning for unknown topics stays.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a Lernkartei: each subject is its own card box, years are tabbed dividers, each material is an index card. Refuses the white portal with two rounded subject cards and icon tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Informatik = deep green box (#2F6B4F) with pale green Karo card stock; Englisch = red box (#C2372B) with yellow lined card stock and a red header rule. Paper-grey ground, black 2px keylines, flat print, no soft shadows. Andika for titles and big year numerals, Nunito for text.

STORY: The visitor sees immediately there are two boxes, pulls the tab of their year, and finds cards grouped under topic headings; new cards have a clipped corner; the last opened card carries a folded flag on return.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: top bar (title, school, search) over two half-width colour fields side by side, each with subject name, tab row 5–12 (years with material raised and active, empty years flat and inert), and the open card stack of the default year. Mobile: subject switch as two coloured tabs, one box at a time.

FORM: Lernkartei, candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:33d60e236e5b0877. Raises: subject colour owns whole regions (Dumbar); oversized year numerals as main wayfinding (brick instructions); state as marks — clipped corner = neu, folded flag = last opened (cutting bench).
